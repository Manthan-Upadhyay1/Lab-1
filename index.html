How it works

Four steps from first message to your pet's first visit — most families are set up within a week.

  1. Tell us about your pet

    A short form on habits, health notes, and any quirks worth knowing before a stranger meets them.

  2. Meet your caregiver

    A free at-home introduction, so your pet gets comfortable before any booking is confirmed.

  3. Book one-off or recurring

    Set a weekly walking schedule, or book boarding and grooming only when you need it.

  4. Get live updates

    A photo, a note, and a route map land in your inbox as soon as each visit wraps up.
